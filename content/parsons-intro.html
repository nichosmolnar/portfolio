Does the length of a movie influence its popularity on Netflix? In partnertship with Netflix, with data from netflix's bi-yearly watch report, I visualized how Netflix movies of different genres are distributed in terms of length and viewership. Observing the relationship between length and viewership allows the user to explore a dataset of thousands of movies in minutes, and brings insight into genre-specific strategies and trends in moviemaking.
